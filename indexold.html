CAFE III in three simple ideas
Sales mix sets fleet weight

More heavy vehicles generally increase average fleet weight. More light vehicles reduce it.

Fleet weight affects the target

The numerical target changes with the sales-weighted average unladen mass of the fleet.

Powertrain mix affects performance

Fuel use, eligible fuel treatment, technology adjustment and special volume weighting affect the calculated result.

What is a rule and what is an assumption?
Notified rule

Year-wise target coefficients, corporate-average equation, specified fuel conversions, CNF treatment, eligible technology adjustment and special volume factors.

Research-informed starting point

Anonymous company types are shaped to resemble common Indian passenger-vehicle portfolio patterns. They are not official OEM compliance datasets.

Modelling assumption

Representative mass and CO₂ or energy-use values assigned to each powertrain.

Your input

Every fuel-share change made using the sliders or example buttons.

Why anonymous examples?

Public sales data can shape realistic examples, but it does not provide every model-level mass, certification result, technology claim or regulatory filing input needed to reproduce an OEM’s actual CAFE position.
