Method, factors and assumptions
W = Σ nᵢWᵢ / Σ nᵢ
Target T = a × (W − 1229) + c
P = Σ vᵢnᵢ [FCᵢ(1 − CNFᵢ) − cᵢ] / Σ vᵢnᵢ
g CO₂/km = L/100 km × 23.7135
Passbook entry = (T − P) g/km × Σnᵢ
  • T and P are rounded to three decimals before comparison. MIDC basis; the WLTP conversion is yet to be notified.
  • Strong hybrid (flex-fuel) takes 8% CNF: the final notification lists 22.3% only for flex-fuel vehicles.
  • PHEV and REEV use declared weighted CO₂ on a petrol basis. Diesel CNF follows the notified biodiesel blend (0% here); CNG uses 5% or the CBG blend, whichever is higher.
  • Tech credits apply to every eligible powertrain; BEVs skip engine-related items (marked *).
  • Year-wise plan (assumption): the mix moves linearly from start to end; CO₂ improvement and sales growth compound yearly.
  • Credits and debits carry forward within a block and lapse at block end. Pooling sums members' block balances; trading terms are not modelled.
  • Portfolios are generic composites of Indian market patterns, not any manufacturer's data.
